Simple decision guide

Start with the problem, not the product.

The best question is not “What peptide should I take?” It is “What am I trying to fix, and what information do we need first?”

GoalHistoryLabsSafety
1 · Main goal

Pick one: weight, gut, skin, injury, energy, sleep, hormones, or focus.

2 · What else is going on?

Training, stress, sleep, medications, past injuries, and health history matter.

3 · What might fit?

Use the chart below to know what topics are worth asking about.

4 · Safety check

Confirm with a qualified provider before using anything.

Simple routing map

  • Gut problems: ask about inflammation and gut-support topics.
  • Skin redness or irritation: ask about skin and inflammation support.
  • Tendon/joint injury: ask about recovery-focused options.
  • Low energy: ask about sleep, thyroid, iron, B12, hormones, and recovery first.
  • Weight/cravings: ask about GLP options, protein, strength training, and follow-up.
  • Poor sleep/recovery: ask whether stress, training load, or hormones are involved.

Labs to bring if you have them

Do not worry if you do not have all of these. Bring anything recent.

  • Basic bloodwork
  • Blood sugar / A1c
  • Cholesterol
  • Thyroid
  • Hormones when relevant
  • Vitamin D, B12, iron/ferritin when relevant

Red flags

  • Do not start anything if you do not know what it is for.
  • Do not ignore side effects.
  • Do not combine multiple things without guidance.
  • Do not skip medical screening for medications or injections.
