Take-home discussion guide

What should I ask about?

Use this as a conversation map. The goal is not to self-prescribe—it is to match your main bottleneck with better questions, labs, and clinician guidance.

10-minute self-checkLabs firstSafety over hype
1 · Goal

Pick the one area you most want help with: weight, gut, skin, injury, energy, sleep, hormones, cognition.

2 · Context

Training load, nutrition, medications, history, labs, and symptoms change the answer.

3 · Category

Discuss categories: GLP, LIPO+, KPV, BPC/TB, GHK-Cu, GH, mitochondrial, immune.

4 · Clinician

Confirm safety, legality, sourcing, dispensing, dose, monitoring, and stop rules.

Quick routing map

  • Gut + skin: ask about KPV / inflammation support and gut barrier basics.
  • Skin quality: ask about GHK-Cu and collagen-supporting habits.
  • Acute tendon/joint issue: ask whether BPC-157/TB-500 category is appropriate.
  • Energy only: do not jump to KPV; ask about labs, sleep, thyroid, iron/B12, mitochondrial category.
  • Appetite / weight / glucose: ask about GLP pathway and LIPO+ support.
  • Sleep + recovery: discuss labs, training load, and GH peptide category only after screening.
  • Longevity / cognition: ask about mitochondrial and neuropeptide categories, not quick fixes.
  • Red flags: clinician-first, labs-first, no protocol guessing.

Safety questions to ask

  • What labs should I get before starting?
  • Who reviews my medications and medical history?
  • What side effects mean stop immediately?
  • Where is it sourced and legally dispensed?
  • How do we track whether it is helping?
  • What lifestyle basics should come first?

Bring these labs if you have them

CBC, CMP, fasting glucose, HbA1c, fasting insulin, lipids, ApoB, thyroid panel, testosterone/free testosterone/SHBG/estradiol when appropriate, IGF-1, hs-CRP, vitamin D, B12, ferritin/iron, and relevant history.
