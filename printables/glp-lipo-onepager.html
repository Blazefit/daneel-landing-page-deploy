What’s in LIPO+?

METHIONINELiver function + fat processing

An amino acid involved in liver function and fat metabolism. It helps the liver metabolize fats and may help reduce fat buildup in the liver.

INOSITOLCell signaling + insulin sensitivity

Supports cell signaling and insulin sensitivity. It plays a role in how the body handles fats and sugars and may support metabolic function.

CHOLINEFat transport out of the liver

Important for transporting fats out of the liver. Without enough choline, fat can accumulate in the liver instead of being processed efficiently.

B VITAMINSCellular energy pathways

Often B12, B6, and B5. These help convert carbohydrates, fats, and proteins into energy at the cellular level.
