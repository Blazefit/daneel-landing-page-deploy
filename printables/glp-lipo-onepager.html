Simple guide

GLP + LIPO+

A simple way to think about it: GLP support may help appetite and cravings. LIPO+ is nutrient support that may help your body handle energy and fat metabolism.

AppetiteCravingsEnergy SupportMedical Oversight

WHAT IT MEANS

Not magic. A support tool.

These options work best when they are part of a real plan: protein, strength training, walking, sleep, hydration, and follow-up labs.

Important: GLP medications require medical screening. They are not right for everyone.

Who should ask?

  • People struggling with appetite or cravings
  • People working on weight loss or body composition
  • People worried about blood sugar or insulin issues
  • People who want a supervised plan, not guesswork

GLP — plain English

GLP conversations are usually about appetite, fullness, cravings, blood sugar, and weight-loss support.

  • May help you feel full sooner
  • May reduce cravings for some people
  • Needs screening, dosing guidance, and follow-up

LIPO+ — plain English

LIPO+ is nutrient support. Think of it as a helper, not the main driver.

  • Methionine: supports liver fat processing
  • Inositol: supports healthy cell signaling
  • Choline: helps move fat out of the liver
  • B vitamins: support energy conversion

Best questions to ask

“Am I a good candidate?” · “What labs should I check first?” · “How do I protect muscle?” · “What side effects should I watch for?” · “How will we track progress?”
