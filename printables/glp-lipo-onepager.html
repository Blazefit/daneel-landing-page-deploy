Plain-English guide

GLP + LIPO+: appetite support + nutrient support.

This sheet explains the conversation simply: GLP options may help some people with appetite and cravings; LIPO+ is nutrient support. Neither replaces food quality, strength training, sleep, or provider oversight.

🍽️

GLP conversation

Usually discussed around appetite, cravings, “food noise,” blood sugar, and weight-loss support. The big question: can you lose fat while protecting muscle?

LIPO+ conversation

A nutrient injection blend often discussed as support for energy metabolism and liver/fat-processing pathways. It is not a magic fat burner.

These questions turn a weight-loss product pitch into a safer plan.

1

Questions to bring to Gulfshore Wellness
  • What side effects should I watch for?
  • What labs do you want before or during?
  • How do we avoid muscle loss?
  • How long would we try this before reassessing?
  • What would make me stop or change course?

CrossFit Blaze lens

The goal is not “smaller at any cost.” The goal is better body composition, better habits, and enough energy to train.

A good plan should support protein, strength training, hydration, digestion, sleep, and regular check-ins.
