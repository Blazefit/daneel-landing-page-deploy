Common starting points

  • Weight / cravings: ask about GLP options and nutrition support.
  • Low energy: ask about sleep, labs, thyroid, iron/B12, and recovery.
  • Gut or skin issues: ask about inflammation support and triggers.
  • Joint or tendon pain: ask about injury-focused recovery options.
  • Sleep / recovery: ask whether training load, stress, or hormones are part of the problem.
